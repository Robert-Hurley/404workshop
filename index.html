What gets built here

404 Workshop is a home for useful, weird, open, and occasionally unnecessary things made with wood, computers, printers, synths, cameras, solder, and stubbornness.

01

Woodworking

Furniture, shop fixtures, storage, jigs, outdoor builds, and one-off pieces designed to be useful first and good-looking second.

02

3D Printing

Functional parts, stands, adapters, fixtures, and designs worth sharing instead of gatekeeping.

03

Music + Electronics

Sampler setups, synth experiments, studio accessories, DIY utilities, and electronic noise with a purpose.

04

Software + Tech

Small tools, scripts, web projects, hardware experiments, and practical technical write-ups.

The thing that started the name.

Open project

Roland SP-404MKII Stand

I wanted a simple stand for the SP-404MKII. Most of what I found online was a finished 3D print for $30–50, so I designed my own instead. The result worked well enough that it made more sense to share the files than become another guy selling a piece of plastic on the internet.

The workshop plan

404 Workshop is meant to grow into a small maker business without turning every hobby into a product launch.

Business plan

Build useful things. Share the process. Sell the work that makes sense.

Start small, keep overhead low, and let real projects decide what becomes a product instead of manufacturing inventory and hoping somebody wants it.

Woodworking projectsSmall furniture, shelves, studio pieces, shop organization, jigs, outdoor projects, and custom one-offs.
Digital maker filesSTLs, CAD files, plans, templates, and documentation—some free, some paid when the design justifies it.
Technical projectsSoftware utilities, electronics, music-workflow tools, and practical build write-ups under the same 404 Workshop name.
How it can earn
01
Finished buildsSell selected woodworking and maker projects locally or by commission.
02
Plans + filesOffer project plans, CAD, STL files, and build documentation with free/open projects alongside paid ones.
03
Custom workTake on the occasional practical problem that fits the workshop instead of becoming a general job shop.
04
Content + supportUse project pages, MakerWorld, GitHub, and optional supporter links to grow the audience without putting everything behind a paywall.
